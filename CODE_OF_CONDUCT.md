# Code of Conduct

HTM brings together communities with different methods, vocabularies, and beliefs. Participation requires respectful, evidence-oriented collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Critique claims, evidence, methods, translations, and code rather than people or communities.
- State assumptions and uncertainty clearly.
- Distinguish historical description from modern interpretation and experimental result.
- Welcome good-faith skepticism, negative findings, and corrections.
- Protect private and sensitive information.
- Give appropriate attribution and credit.
- Avoid harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, personal attacks, and unwanted disclosure of private information.

## Research disagreements

Support for or skepticism toward traditional knowledge is not a license to dismiss contributors. Historical authority does not settle an empirical question, and scientific vocabulary does not excuse inaccurate representation of historical sources. Contributors should make disagreements testable whenever possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove content that violates this code and may restrict participation for repeated or serious violations. For sensitive reports, contact the repository owner through the contact information on the owner's GitHub profile rather than posting private details publicly.
